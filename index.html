Lake Reads Chitlapakkam

A silent reading community that meets every Saturday at the Chitlapakkam lake in South Chennai (without a break for the last 2.5 years). Open and free for readers who are new to reading and knee-deep in Literature.

Readers bring a book, sit, read for an hour by the lake, discuss till they run out of creative juice and blood glucose (that's when we go for breakfast where the discussion continues).

Check out our Insta page

The community also brought out Readers Lakelet, a collection of essays, short stories and poems written by its readers.

What We Read

On AI

I am both an advocate and a devil's advocate. I believe technology must progress, but not by trading away human sensitivities, sensibilities, and cognition to LLMs in the form of tokens.

Reading, writing, and other forms of expression are what make us empathetic and humane. We must be mindful not to delegate them to AI in the name of technological evolution.
